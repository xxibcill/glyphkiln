## Fixed copy

Preserve exactly, including line breaks shown as `↵` where specified.

- 1 `Choose one workflow.`
- 2 `Map its input, steps and output.`
- 3 `Mark one decision that needs human review.`
- 4 `Define a small pilot and its exclusions.`
- 5 `Compare observed results with the starting condition.`
- 6 `Decide: revise, stop or expand.`
