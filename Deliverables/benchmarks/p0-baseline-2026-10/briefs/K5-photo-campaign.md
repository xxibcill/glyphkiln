# K5 — Three-format photo campaign

[All briefs](README.md) · [Scoring sheet](scoring-sheet.md)

| Field                             | Value                                                                                     |
| --------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Viewer task                       | Recognize the lamp and the brand in three placements                                      |
| Message                           | Kilnform's cobalt ceramic lamp turns clay into glow.                                      |
| Relationship that should carry it | Material ↔ light (clay to glow), with each format recomposed for its shape                |
| Wrong interpretation to avoid     | A generic interior or lighting ad where the brand is lost                                 |
| Canvas / format                   | LinkedIn 1200×627; Instagram 1080×1080; Instagram 1080×1350                               |
| Delivery view                     | 360 px wide for all three                                                                 |
| Proposed time box                 | 2 hours for all three                                                                     |
| Comprehension question            | “Which brand is this, and what is the product?” Correct: Kilnform; a cobalt ceramic lamp. |

## Fixed copy

Preserve exactly, including line breaks shown as `↵` where specified.

- LinkedIn headline: `Make room for the glow.`
- Instagram square and portrait headline: `Clay to↵glow.` (two lines)
- Caption (outside the image, for reference): `The Studio Lamp returns in cobalt ceramic. A sculptural pool of light for the corners you keep. Meet the Second Firing from Kilnform.`

## Assets

- Lamp photograph `kilnform-lamp-campaign.png` (`42930ab3…`) — the only photograph
- Kilnform mark `kilnform-mark.png` (`4c582c95…`)
- Inter Variable (`29160a80…`)
- Palette: primary `#0D3B9C` (cobalt), secondary `#C85F27` (clay), accent `#F3C879`, neutrals `#15100D`, `#FFF6E7`, `#A77B5A`; dark theme surface `#2B211C`, light surface `#F3E4CC`.

## Missing inputs

- A light or monochrome variant of the Kilnform mark for dark/photographic grounds (only the cobalt variant exists). Designers may not redraw the mark; until a variant is supplied, both sides use the existing mark.

## Mandatory defects (any one fails the entry)

1. Any change, omission, or reordering of the fixed copy
2. Text that cannot be read at the delivery view size (judged on the delivered-size proof, not the master)
3. Text clipped, overflowing, or colliding with another element
4. Product silhouette cropped through a defining feature (lamp shade, cobalt base, finial) where the product appears
5. An invented claim, testimonial, statistic, price, or product feature
6. Kilnform mark not identifiable at 360 px
7. Cobalt base visibly desaturated or darkened compared with the source photograph
8. The three formats are the same layout resized (each must use its own composition)
