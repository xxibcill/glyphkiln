## Missing inputs

## Mandatory defects (any one fails the entry)

1. Any change, omission, or reordering of the fixed copy
2. Text that cannot be read at the delivery view size (judged on the delivered-size proof, not the master)
3. Text clipped, overflowing, or colliding with another element
4. Product silhouette cropped through a defining feature (lamp shade, cobalt base, finial) where the product appears
5. An invented claim, testimonial, statistic, price, or product feature
6. Title unreadable at 320 px

## Notes

At thumbnail sizes the byline may be dropped from the image only if it moves to the page metadata; record the choice.
