# P0 baseline critique ledger — Kilnform designer stress test

**Reviewed 22 October 2026 by Claude (Opus 5.5) for the project owner. Status:
internal design review; no audience, comprehension, or commercial evidence.**

This ledger is phase P0 of the
[design-quality improvement plan](../../../docs/plans/design-quality-improvement-plan.md).
It records the "before" state of the 15 graphics in the
[designer stress test](../../designer-stress-test-2026-09-25/README.md) so that
later phases can be compared against the same files, sizes, and copy.

## Scope and method

- **Artifacts:** the exact PNG outputs in
  [`outputs/`](../../designer-stress-test-2026-09-25/outputs/) and
  [`scene/how-the-light-is-made.png`](../../designer-stress-test-2026-09-25/scene/how-the-light-is-made.png),
  as committed on branch `codex/reference-led-design-refinement`. Versions are
  given per entry as `template@version`.
- **Sizes inspected:** full output size, and a deterministic box-filtered copy
  at a **360 px phone feed width** in [`phone-width/`](phone-width/)
  (regenerate with `make-phone-previews.mjs`). The phone copy is a proportional
  reduction, not an actual device or platform capture.
- **Rubric:** seven dimensions reviewed separately (GD19, revised): concept /
  message fit, composition, typography, imagery, color, coherence, finish.
  No dimension is averaged into a score.
- **Finding format:** `version + size + region → observation → consequence →
likely cause → smallest useful repair → constraints to preserve`, classified
  as **concept**, **craft**, **preference**, or **evidence gap**.
- **Kinds of claim are kept apart.** _Technical_: what Core checked and passed.
  _Craft_: this reviewer's visual judgment. _Comprehension_ and _commercial_
  claims are **not made**: nothing here was tested with viewers.
- **Advisory heuristics:** each entry also cites the deterministic craft
  review (policy `craft-review 1.0.0`, see
  [quality policy](../../../docs/quality-policy.md#advisory-craft-review)),
  stored in [`craft-review.json`](craft-review.json) and regenerated by
  `run-craft-review.mjs`. Where reviewer judgment and a heuristic disagree,
  both are recorded.

Fixed facts for every entry: copy, brand palette (`#F3C879` accent), the
supplied lamp photograph (`kilnform-lamp-campaign.png`, sha256 `42930ab3…`),
the Kilnform mark (`4c582c95…`), and Inter Variable (`29160a80…`) are the
inputs; the brief and all claims are fictional.

**Overall baseline judgment (craft, reviewer):** the set is technically clean
and consistent, and the photo campaign and process diagram are usable first
directions. The remaining graphics read as one template family rather than a
Kilnform idea. The recurring causes are (1) no subject-specific visual premise,
(2) fixed containers that do not respond to the amount of copy, (3) supporting
type sized for the canvas rather than the delivery size, and (4) an accent used
as decoration rather than as a signal. These map to plan workstreams W1, W2,
W3, and W5.

## Heuristic calibration notes

What this baseline shows about the advisory checks themselves:

- **Agreement:** the dead-space check found the two panels a reviewer flags
  first (quote, statistic) and the empty carousel fields; the small-text check
  found every label the reviewer judged unreadable at phone width.
- **Silence is not a pass:** the campaign has no findings, yet its main
  defects (unrecomposed formats, lost mark, dimmed product color) are outside
  what the four checks measure. Mark/subject visibility (W4) and a recomposition
  check are needed.
- **Defensible disagreement:** the process diagram's accent finding (PD-3) is
  a consistent role, not overuse. The usage count already merges same-size
  repeats; consistent multi-size roles still trigger it. Keep the check
  advisory and record exceptions.
- **Delivery width is an input, not a constant:** the landscape announcement is
  clean at 600 px but not at 360 px. Briefs must state their delivery width
  (see `briefs/`).
- **Scale contrast never fired.** Kilnform's problem is not competing sizes but
  unused space and missing imagery; the check remains useful for denser
  layouts (offers, comparisons).

## What this baseline does not establish

No viewer was shown these graphics. Nothing here measures comprehension,
preference, recall, or commercial performance, and no professional designer
has yet produced the comparison set. The blind comparison protocol and the
twelve fixed briefs are in [`briefs/`](briefs/) and
[`briefs/scoring-sheet.md`](briefs/scoring-sheet.md).
