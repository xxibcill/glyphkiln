# Kilnform process diagram — designer review

## Brief and visual decision

An editorial 1200 × 675 process diagram for fictional ceramic lighting brand Kilnform. The left-to-right four-card sequence gives each stage a distinct schematic drawing and one short explanation. Warm paper, clay accents, dark ink, and a restrained blue-green process rail keep the craft story coherent. The rail arrows clarify order without competing with the stage headings.

## Limits before use

The process drawings are schematic. A Kilnform maker should check the stage wording against the actual lamp, clay body, firing, glaze, electrical assembly, and safety test. The wordmark and small K device are diagram typography, not a claim to reproduce an approved Kilnform identity asset. Scene Kernel does not score visual quality, truth, or commercial legibility; this review is for the supplied landscape size only. No print, PDF, CMYK, or publisher-specific derivative is included.
