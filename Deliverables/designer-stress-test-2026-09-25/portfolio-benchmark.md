# External graphic-design benchmark

**Reviewed:** 25 September 2026. This is a visual critique of the Kilnform
stress-test outputs against specific public Behance and Dribbble projects. The
references are selected portfolio presentations, not a representative sample
of everyday agency work or evidence of campaign performance. Kilnform is a
fictional brief with one supplied product photograph.

## Capability finding

Glyphkiln passed this run's deterministic rendering and provenance checks. The
external comparison exposes a different threshold: a senior designer must
develop a subject-specific visual concept, vary the story across placements,
and inspect the result at its actual viewing size. The current semantic
templates support consistency and reproducibility but constrained the organic
carousel to text-only composition. The Scene Kernel allowed a more bespoke
diagram, though it required code-level authoring and manual truth review.

The next creative iteration should: (1) admit product imagery in organic
carousel layouts; (2) explore at least three materially different campaign
compositions with a second rights-cleared product or lifestyle image; (3)
derive a type or graphic motif from the lamp's form; and (4) validate the
process diagram against a real maker before treating it as informational art.
These are design and product tests, not claims that the reference projects had
better commercial results. Their project pages do not establish client
approval, accessibility, export quality, or reproducibility.
