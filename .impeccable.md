## Design Context

### Users

Glyphkiln App serves self-hosting product, brand, and marketing teams—plus the
developers who operate the tool—who need to turn structured content and brand
rules into dependable business and social graphics. Their first job is to
define a brand snapshot, produce a schema-valid design document, understand any
validation issues, inspect the exact Core-rendered preview, and deliberately
download SVG, PNG, and provenance outputs without relying on an LLM.

### Brand Personality

Precise, craft-forward, and trustworthy. The interface should make deterministic
rendering feel understandable and controlled while retaining the tactile,
purposeful character suggested by the Glyphkiln name. The emotional goal is
quiet confidence: users should feel that they can see what the system is doing
and reproduce the result.

### Aesthetic Direction

Use a light-first industrial-editorial workshop aesthetic: warm paper-like
surfaces, ink-toned typography, restrained kiln/clay accents, crisp rules, and
strong typographic hierarchy. Prefer composed, asymmetric working layouts over
generic dashboard card grids. The visual language should feel like a serious
print studio translated to software, not a neon generative-AI product,
glassmorphic control panel, or playful freeform design toy.

The application must remain responsive, keyboard accessible, legible at high
zoom, usable with reduced motion, and clear without depending on color alone.

### Design Principles

1. Show the contract: keep structured inputs, validation, render status, and
   provenance legible instead of hiding the deterministic pipeline.
2. Make the canvas the focal artifact while keeping controls compact,
   progressive, and clearly grouped.
3. Use material warmth and editorial typography with restraint; decoration must
   reinforce the kiln/workshop metaphor or the current task.
4. Preserve trust through explicit actions and states—preview is not save, and
   every export format is chosen deliberately.
5. Adapt the workspace across screen sizes without removing validation,
   preview, or export capabilities.
